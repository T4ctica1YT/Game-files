Melee Web

Super Smash Bros. Melee, compiled from the decompilation to WebAssembly and drawn with WebGPU. It runs from your own copy of the game, which never leaves this computer.

Your disc

Looking for a disc…

Super Smash Bros. Melee NTSC 1.02 (GALE01), as a plain .iso or .gcm. A disc image placed in this page's folder loads by itself.

Settings

Performance

For slow devices, the low-end preset renders at a lower resolution and draws every other frame while the game itself keeps full speed.

Anti-aliasing and texture filtering change when the game restarts.

Controls
